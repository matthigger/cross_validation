Cross validation, visually

How well does a model do on penguins it has never seen? Estimate it with the penguins you have.

k-fold cross validation

Split the samples into k folds. Each round, train the model on k−1 folds (training) and use it to estimate the one fold left out (testing). After k rounds every sample has exactly one estimate, made by a model that never saw it.

Each penguin i has a label yi: y = 0 Adelie y = 1 Chinstrap y = 2 Gentoo and an estimate ŷi, one of the same three.
CV accuracy = 1n ∑i 1[ ŷi = yi ]
Each penguin i has a label yi: yi = body mass (g) and an estimate ŷi from its flipper length. The error is yi − ŷi.
CV MSE = 1n ∑i (yi − ŷi)2

Here ŷi always comes from the round where sample i was testing.

Try this

  • Step through every round: each penguin is testing exactly once, and its estimate stays put afterwards.
  • Switch to Sorted by species with k = 3. Each testing fold is one species, and that species is missing from training. The model cannot estimate a species it never saw, so every estimate is wrong.
  • Back to Shuffled, set k = 2 and reshuffle a few times. Most orders are fine, but some leave a fold short of one species.
  • Choose Stratified: every fold gets the same mix of species (as near as k allows), so no reshuffle can leave a species out of training.
  • Watch the line from round to round: it moves a little, because each round fits it on different penguins. A testing penguin far from its line gets a tall error bar.
  • Set k = 12: leave-one-out. Twelve rounds, each testing one penguin, and the result no longer depends on the order.
