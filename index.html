k-fold cross validation

Split the samples into k folds. Each round, train the model on k−1 folds (training) and use it to estimate the one fold left out (testing). After k rounds every sample has exactly one estimate, made by a model that never saw it.

Model: nearest centroid. Average the bill length and bill depth of each species in the training folds; estimate a penguin as the species whose average is closest.

CV accuracy = 1n ∑i 1[ ŷi = yi ]

Model: least squares line, body mass ≈ β0 + β1 · flipper length, fit on the training folds. Each estimate's error is yi − ŷi (actual minus estimate).

CV MSE = 1n ∑i (yi − ŷi)2

Here ŷi always comes from the round where sample i was testing.

Try this

  • Step through every round: each penguin is testing exactly once, and its estimate stays put afterwards.
  • Switch to Sorted by species with k = 3. Each testing fold is one species, and that species is missing from training. The model cannot estimate a species it never saw, so every estimate is wrong. The line must extrapolate to penguins unlike any it was fit on, so the errors grow.
  • Back to Shuffled, set k = 2 and reshuffle a few times. Most orders are fine, but some leave a fold short of one species. Stratified folds avoid this by giving every fold the same mix of species.
  • Set k = 12: leave-one-out. Twelve rounds, each testing one penguin, and the result no longer depends on the order.
